# praemonitor

Follow [the functional contract](../orchestrator/SKILL.md). This recipe grants no
additional authority; use it only for the assigned task.

Model a specific failure chain, rank plausible risks and identify early signals and bounded mitigations. Return one analysis to the lead; do not open a debate loop or assign parallel agents. Do not turn hypothetical improvements into required implementation.
