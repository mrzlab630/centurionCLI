## Synthesized Lessons

1. UX-writing quality comes from a system, not one-off clever phrases.
2. Voice is stable; tone changes by user state.
3. Good microcopy is actionable: it names what happens and what the user can do next.
4. Empty/error states are product moments, not filler text.
5. Word lists and glossaries prevent drift across screens, teams, and locales.
6. Plain language and accessibility are part of product quality.
7. Copy examples should be adapted to the actual product and tested in the UI.
